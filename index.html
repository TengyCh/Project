About

I'm a first-generation college student currently at College of Lake County, working toward a transfer into a Computer Science program for Fall 2027. Before this, I grew up speaking Mongolian at home, learned English, and picked up Japanese along the way — I hold an Honorable Mention on the National Japanese Examination and I'm part of the Japanese National Honor Society.

I've spent the last few years as a math tutor — I lead the tutor team at MGL Math Center and TA for math and social studies at my high school — and for years before that I competed as a floor gymnast. Both taught me the same thing in different ways: show up, drill the fundamentals, and the hard parts eventually click.

Right now I'm learning Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Supabase to build real full-stack projects with a small team, while also serving as a Transfer Student Ambassador — helping other students navigate the exact process I'm going through myself.
